# Login page photo

Drop your image here and name it exactly:

    login-photo.jpg

(If your file is a .png or .webp instead, either rename it to end in .jpg,
or open src/pages/Login.jsx and change the `src="/images/login-photo.jpg"`
line to match your actual filename/extension.)

## Where to get a photo you're legally allowed to use

Free, no-attribution-required stock photo sites:
- https://unsplash.com — search "students computer lab" or "online exam"
- https://www.pexels.com — same idea, same license terms
- https://pixabay.com — another good option

Avoid grabbing a random image from Google Images search results — most of
those are still under someone else's copyright even without a watermark.

## What happens if there's no image here

The login page automatically falls back to a custom illustration if
login-photo.jpg is missing or fails to load — so nothing breaks if you
haven't added a photo yet.

## Sizing tips

The image displays at roughly 420px wide with a 4:3 aspect ratio (cropped
to fill, not stretched) — so a landscape-orientation photo works best.
Anything reasonably high-resolution (at least 800px wide) will look sharp.
